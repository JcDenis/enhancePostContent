Filter
Choose in the list the filter to set up. Each filter adds a feature to words found in your posts: abbreviation, citation, link, tag, replacement, etc.
Settings
Choose the pages and the contents where the filter works, how words are matched, how many replacements are made, the CSS style and the HTML tags to ignore.
Records
List of the words of the filter: the key is the word to find, the value is its explanation, link or replacement. Select records to delete them.
New record
Add a word to the filter, with its key and its value.
Blog settings
The plugin must be enabled in the blog settings, in the plugins parameters, to filter contents and to show its widget.
